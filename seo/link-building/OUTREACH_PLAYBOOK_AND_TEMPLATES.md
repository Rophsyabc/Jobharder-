# JobHardER — Ethical Outreach Playbook & Pitch Templates

**Target Site:** [JobHardER](https://www.jobharder.com/)  
**Campaign Phase:** Phase 2 Link Building & Topical Authority  
**Standard:** 100% Personalised, Value-First, White-Hat Editorial Outreach

---

## 1. Ethical Rules of Engagement

Every outreach communication from JobHardER must reflect professional editorial integrity. 

### Mandatory Standards:
* ❌ **Never send mass automated email blasts:** Every outreach communication must be personally researched and individually addressed.
* ❌ **Never write "Dear Webmaster" or "Dear Sir/Madam":** If a specific department head, career officer, or editor's name or title cannot be identified, the prospect must be researched further before contact.
* ❌ **Never use deceptive urgency:** No countdown timers, "urgent partnership opportunities," or fake sponsorship claims.
* ❌ **Never offer financial compensation for editorial references:** True authority is built on organic utility, not paid link schemes.
* ❌ **Never demand or pressure for a backlink:** We suggest helpful resources. The recipient retains total editorial discretion to reference, ignore, or decline.
* ❌ **Strict follow-up limit:** Maximum of **one polite follow-up** sent 7–10 days after initial contact. If there is no response, mark as `No Response` and gracefully move on.

---

## 2. Pre-Outreach Personalisation Checklist (Step 7)

Before drafting any outreach email, complete this 5-point verification:

1. **Exact Organisation & Department:** Have you identified the specific unit (e.g., *Career Services Centre*, *Directorate of Student Affairs*, *Employability Desk*)?
2. **Contact Identification:** Do you have the specific officer's name, title, or dedicated unit email (e.g., `careerservices@institution.edu.ng`)?
3. **Existing Resource Identification:** What specific page, guide, or student advisory post on their website already deals with this topic?
4. **Relevance Justification:** Why does the proposed JobHardER resource provide distinct, updated, or complementary value to their students/members?
5. **Exact Contextual Destination:** Where on their platform would this naturally fit (e.g., *Student Career Resources links*, *Weekly Student Bulletin*, *Alumni Transition Toolkit*)?
