## Overview

Natural, high-authority backlinks cannot be requested without offering genuine reference value. Webmasters, university career officers, and journalists link to **definitive calendars, consumer-protection tools, verified salary benchmarks, and comprehensive application toolkits**. 

This roadmap outlines the architecture, data structures, update cadences, and editorial standards for JobHardER's 5 core linkable evergreen resources.

## Resource A: 2026 Nigerian Graduate Trainee & Internship Calendar

* **Professional Services & Consulting:** PwC Nigeria, KPMG Nigeria, Ernst & Young (EY), Deloitte West Africa, Andersen Nigeria.
* **Engineering, Logistics & Construction:** Dangote Industries, BUA Group, Julius Berger Nigeria, DHL Nigeria.
* **Public Sector & International NGOs:** Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN EDP), Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS), Bank of Industry (BOI), United Nations Development Programme (UNDP Nigeria), International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC).

### 3. Structural Status Protocol (Mandatory Distinction)
Every program listed in this calendar must carry one of four explicit visual status indicators. **Never present an old or recurring programme as currently open:**

| Status Label | Color Tag | Definition | Actionable Details Required |
|--------------|-----------|------------|-----------------------------|
| **OPEN** | `Green` | Applications are currently being accepted on the employer's official portal. | Closing deadline, official portal link, eligible graduation year. |
| **UPCOMING** | `Yellow` | Officially announced by the employer or recruitment partner, opening soon. | Expected opening date, prep checklist, notification signup. |
| **CLOSED** | `Gray` | Application deadline has elapsed for the current cycle. | Date closed, expected date of next annual cycle. |
| **EXPECTED / RECURRING** | `Blue` | Historically recurring annual recruitment with known historical window. | Historical announcement month (e.g. "Historically opens Q2 (April–May)"). |

### 4. Required Schema for Each Entry
```markdown
### [Employer Name] Graduate Trainee Programme [Year]
- **Status:** [OPEN / UPCOMING / CLOSED / EXPECTED]
- **Target Disciplines:** [Engineering / Sciences / Social Sciences / Open to All]
- **Minimum Qualification:** [First Class / 2:1 / 2:2 / HND Upper Credit]
- **Age Limit & NYSC Requirement:** [e.g., Max 26 years; NYSC Discharge or Exemption Certificate]
- **Application Window:** [Opening Date] — [Closing Date]
- **Aptitude Test Format:** [Dragnet / SHL / Workforce / In-house CBT]
- **Official Application Source:** [Direct Link to Employer's Official Careers Portal]
*(JobHardER Advisory: Never pay any recruitment fee. Legitimate Nigerian corporate graduate schemes never charge applicants.)*
```

## Resource B: Nigerian Job Scam Verification Guide (Public-Interest Flagship)

### 1. Strategic Purpose
A public-interest consumer-protection guide designed to protect Nigerian job seekers from fraudulent recruitment syndicates, advance-fee scams, and fake employment schemes. This is JobHardER's primary asset for university career desks, campus journalists, and consumer rights advocates.

### 2. Core Modules & Forensic Evidence
1. **The Fraudulent Physical Location Register:**
   * Notorious physical syndicates operating under commercial guises in **Maryland, Ikeja (Allen Avenue / Toyin Street / Awolowo Way), Anthony Village, Ojuelegba, Surulere, and Port Harcourt (Aba Road / Trans-Amadi)**.
   * Modus operandi: Receiving SMS interview invitations for jobs never applied for; arriving to discover GNLD/MLM seminar sales pitches, product purchases, or upfront "processing fees."
2. **Digital Verification Checklist (Red Flags):**
   * Free email domains (`@gmail.com`, `@yahoo.com`, `@outlook.com`) claiming to represent Tier-1 multinational oil corporations, banks, or federal ministries.
   * WhatsApp/Telegram-only interviews with immediate "guaranteed employment offers."
   * Requests for payment under any pretext: "Medical clearance fee," "Uniform fee," "Aptitude test booking fee," or "Stamp duty."
   * Spoofed clone domains mimicking legitimate recruiters (e.g., adding extra hyphens or non-standard TLDs like `.site`, `.top`, or `.buzz`).
3. **Official Employer Verification Steps:**
   * Checking Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) public search portal (`search.cac.gov.ng`).
   * Verifying contact emails against official enterprise MX records.
   * Looking up verified enterprise numbers on official social handles.
4. **Official Institutional Reporting Channels:**
   * **EFCC (Economic and Financial Crimes Commission):** Scam reporting email & Eagle Eye App.
   * **Nigeria Police Force Cybercrime Centre (NPF-NCCC):** Online reporting portal.
   * **Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC):** Consumer complaint registry.

## Resource C: Nigerian CV & Job Application Toolkit

### 1. Strategic Purpose
A comprehensive, actionable career toolkit specifically tailored to the formatting standards, cultural expectations, and Applicant Tracking System (ATS) parameters used by Nigerian recruiters and multinational subsidiaries.

### 2. Core Modules
* **The Nigerian Standard CV Architecture:**
  * Contact block (Lagos/Abuja location format, professional email, LinkedIn, GitHub/Portfolio).
  * Professional summary (quantifiable achievements rather than vague adjectives).
  * Educational background (university, degree classification, year of graduation).
  * NYSC Experience presentation (positioning PPA experience as genuine professional contributions).
  * Work experience (Action Verb + Context + Metric formula).
  * Referees policy ("Available on request" vs corporate reference guidelines).
* **ATS Compatibility Checklist for Nigeria:**
  * Single-column formatting vs decorative multi-column templates that crash ATS parsers.
  * Standard section header conventions (`Professional Experience`, `Education`, `Skills`).
  * File formats: PDF vs DOCX guidelines for Dragnet and Taleo systems.
* **The "First Job With No Experience" Blueprint:**
  * Transforming academic capstone projects, SIWES / industrial training, student union leadership, and volunteer work into high-impact CV bullet points.
* **Cover Letter Templates:**
  * Direct application letter to Nigerian corporate recruiters.
  * Cold outreach email template to HR directors on LinkedIn.
* **Aptitude Test Preparation Primer:**
  * Quantitative, Verbal, and Abstract Reasoning guide tailored to Dragnet, SHL, Workforce, and TalentQL question models.

## Resource D: Nigeria Career Salary & Compensation Guide

### 1. Strategic Purpose
To provide transparent, reality-grounded remuneration benchmarks across Nigerian sectors without publishing unsupported or misleading universal numbers.

### 2. Methodology & Sourcing Transparency
* **Reported Salary Examples:** Anonymously submitted, verified compensation figures from active employees.
* **Job-Advertised Ranges:** Base salary bands explicitly listed in verified public job postings.
* **Official Remuneration Guidelines:** Public sector benchmarks (CONPSS, CONTISS, CONMESS) and statutory minimum wage provisions.
* **Limitations Disclaimer:** Explains currency fluctuations, cost-of-living differences between Lagos/Abuja and other states, gross vs. net calculations, statutory deductions (PITA tax, Pension Reform Act 8%, NHF 2.5%, and Health Insurance).

### 3. Sectoral Coverage
* **Commercial & Merchant Banking:** Trainee / Graduate Associate, Executive Trainee, Banking Officer levels.
* **Software Engineering & Technology:** Junior Developer, QA Engineer, UI/UX Designer, Product Manager, DevOps Engineer.
* **FMCG & Manufacturing:** Management Trainee, Shift Engineer, Brand Specialist, Quality Control Officer.
* **Professional Services (Audit & Tax):** Associate 1, Associate 2, Senior Associate.
* **Civil Service & Parastatals:** Entry-level grade levels (GL 08 for university graduates, GL 07 for HND graduates).

## Resource E: Nigeria Scholarships, Internships & Career Opportunities Verification Hub

### 1. Strategic Purpose
An authoritative central directory that aggregates, verifies, and fact-checks undergraduate, postgraduate, and youth fellowship funding opportunities.

### 2. Categorization Structure
* **Undergraduate Scholarships:** NNPC/Chevron National Joint Venture, MTN Foundation Science & Technology, TotalEnergies National Scholarship, Agbami Medical & Engineering Scholarship, Jim Ovia Foundation Leaders Scholarship.
* **Postgraduate & Overseas Grants:** PTDF Overseas & Local Scholarship Scheme, Federal Scholarship Board (FSB) Bilateral Education Agreement (BEA), Chevening (Nigeria cohort), Commonwealth Scholarships, DAAD, Mastercard Foundation Scholars.
* **Fellowships & Youth Skills Initiatives:** Teach For Nigeria Fellowship, Tony Elumelu Foundation Entrepreneurship Programme, MTN Media Innovation Programme, LEAP Africa Social Innovators Programme.

### 3. Fact-Checking Criteria for Every Listing
1. **Official Entity Verification:** Must be backed by a verified corporate foundation, governmental body, or accredited university.
2. **Direct Official URL:** Never link through third-party redirectors; always link to the primary `.gov.ng`, `.org.ng`, or official corporate domain.
3. **Application Fee Prohibition:** Zero tolerance for any scholarship demanding "application voucher fees" or "examination registration charges."
4. **Maintenance & Archival:** Active verification date stamped on every entry. Expired opportunities automatically tagged with `[ARCHIVED / CYCLE CLOSED]` to maintain user trust.
