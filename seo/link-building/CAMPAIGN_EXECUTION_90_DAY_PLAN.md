# JobHardER — 90-Day Link Building & Authority Campaign Plan

**Target Site:** [JobHardER](https://www.jobharder.com/)  
**Campaign Phase:** Phase 2 Link Building & Topical Authority  
**Timeline:** 90 Days (Days 1–30, Days 31–60, Days 61–90)  
**Standard:** 100% Value-Driven, Editorial Merit, Zero Spam

---

## Strategic Philosophy

The objective is **not** to amass arbitrary numbers of low-grade backlinks. The objective is to make **JobHardER an authoritative, reference-worthy resource that Nigerian educational, career, and professional organisations have a legitimate reason to cite**.

Every prospective link must pass the fundamental test:
> *"Would this link still make complete sense if Google did not exist?"*  
> If **YES** → Pursue with care and professional personalisation.  
> If **NO** → Do not pursue.

## Days 1–30: Asset Build, Cluster Integration & Initial Outreach

### Goal:
Deploy the 5 cornerstone linkable assets on JobHardER, establish the 4 internal topical clusters, and execute personalized outreach to the first cohort of verified Nigerian prospects.

### Weekly Breakdown:

#### Week 1: Asset Authoring & Internal Link Cluster Mapping
* [ ] Finalize and publish **Resource A** (*2026 Nigerian Graduate Trainee & Internship Calendar*), verifying that all active schemes link strictly to official corporate portals.
* [ ] Finalize and publish **Resource B** (*Nigerian Job Scam Verification & Anti-Fraud Guide*), complete with known fraudulent addresses in Maryland/Ikeja and official EFCC/NPF reporting steps.
* [ ] Finalize and publish **Resource C** (*Nigerian CV & Job Application Toolkit*), featuring Dragnet/SHL ATS formatting checklists.
* [ ] Execute the internal linking matrix across all 4 clusters (Job Search, Career Development, Salary, and Students/Graduates), ensuring 3–6 contextual links per article with descriptive, varied anchor text.

#### Week 2: Database Preparation & Pre-Outreach Research
* [ ] Finalize and publish **Resource D** (*Nigeria Career Salary & Compensation Guide*) and **Resource E** (*Scholarships & Opportunities Verification Hub*).
* [ ] Audit the 100-prospect tracking ledger (`/seo/link-building/link_prospects.csv`).
* [ ] Conduct pre-outreach research for the first 25 target university career centres (UNILAG, UI, OAU, FUTA, FUTMINNA, LASUSTECH, UNILORIN, FPTB, PLASU, Redeemer's, KWASU, etc.).
* [ ] Identify specific career directors, unit coordinators, and exact student advisory pages on their websites.

#### Week 3: Personalized Outreach Wave 1 (Universities & Professional Bodies)
* [ ] Send personalized outreach to 25 verified University & Polytechnic Career Centres using **Template 1**.
* [ ] Send personalized outreach to 15 verified Professional & Youth Organisations (CIPM, ICAN, NIM, COREN, NYSC CDS desks, FATE Foundation, LEAP Africa) using **Template 2**.
* [ ] Log all outreach dates and notes in `/seo/link-building/link_prospects.csv`.
* [ ] Target: Initiate **10–15 genuine, high-quality professional conversations**.

#### Week 4: Personalized Outreach Wave 2 & First Editorial Pitches
* [ ] Send personalized outreach to remaining 27 university/polytechnic career centres.
* [ ] Send targeted guest editorial pitch to **simLagos** (*"How Nigerian Graduates Can Build Work Experience Before Their First Full-Time Job"*), adhering to their 1,000+ words, original, fact-checked requirement.
* [ ] Send single, polite follow-ups to non-responders from Week 3 (adhering strictly to the 7–10 day follow-up rule).
* [ ] Target: Total of **20–30 active professional outreach conversations** across Month 1.

Expand relationships into student leadership bodies, alumni associations, national career columns, and regional technology portals while publishing quarterly data updates.

* [ ] Submit the complete, original draft to **simLagos** upon pitch acceptance.

* [ ] Send update notifications to university career officers who previously engaged, informing them of the freshly updated calendar.

## Days 61–90: Performance Audit, Asset Doubling & Cadence Institutionalization

### Goal:
Analyse data from Google Search Console, server logs, and outreach records to determine which resources earned the highest editorial engagement, double down on winning formats, and establish an ongoing monthly cadence.

### Weekly Breakdown:

#### Week 9: Exhaustive Backlink & Referral Data Audit
* [ ] Export Google Search Console "Links" report (Top linking sites and Top linked landing pages).
* [ ] Reconcile earned backlinks against `/seo/link-building/link_prospects.csv`.
* [ ] Measure referral traffic quality: Average session duration, pages per session, and bounce rate of visitors arriving from university portals vs news media.

#### Week 10: Topic & Format Performance Analysis
* [ ] Identify which asset generated the highest organic referral and backlink velocity:
  * Did **Resource B** (Scam Guide) win more university citations?
  * Did **Resource A** (Trainee Calendar) win more student and business press citations?
  * Did **Resource D** (Salary Guide) win more industry mentions?
* [ ] Identify underperforming prospect segments and cease low-yield outreach categories.

#### Week 11: Doubling Down on Winning Resource Types
* [ ] Expand the top-performing asset into deeper sub-resources (e.g., if Trainee Calendar is the top asset, build sector-specific deep dives for Banking Trainees and Oil & Gas Trainees).
* [ ] Re-engage verified partner institutions with new seasonal assets (e.g., NYSC Batch B Orientation Guide, Q3 Trainee Assessment Season).

#### Week 12: Campaign Review & Standard Operating Cadence
* [ ] Compile the complete 90-Day Authority & Backlink Summary Report using the standard template.
* [ ] Transition from initial campaign mode into sustainable business-as-usual monthly outreach:
  * 15–20 high-value personalized outreaches per month.
  * Monthly Trainee Calendar and Scholarship verification audits.
  * Quarterly editorial guest contributions to reputable Nigerian business/tech press.

## 90-Day Success Criteria (Quality-First Scorecard)

| Dimension | Target Benchmark | How It Is Measured |
|-----------|------------------|--------------------|
| **Link Quality** | 100% Contextual & Editorial | Every link is embedded in relevant educational or career copy. |
| **Institutional Citations** | 15–30 Authoritative Links | Direct citations from `.edu.ng`, `.org.ng`, or recognized Nigerian news portals. |
| **Outreach Conversations** | 40–60 Meaningful Dialogues | Constructive replies from career officers, editors, and youth leaders. |
| **Referral Traffic Growth** | +25% High-Engagement Visitors | Measurable visits from university career resource portals and reference guides. |
| **Organic Visibility** | Top 5 Rankings for Priority Terms | Page 1 rankings for competitive terms around Nigerian graduate trainees, job scam safety, and CV standards. |
| **Spam / Risk Factor** | **Zero** | Zero paid links, zero PBNs, zero directory spam, 100% white-hat. |
