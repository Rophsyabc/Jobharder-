# JobHardER — Internal Linking Map & Topical Cluster Architecture

**Target Site:** [JobHardER](https://www.jobharder.com/)  
**Campaign Phase:** Phase 2 Link Building & Topical Authority  
**Standard:** Editorial Relevance, 3–6 Contextual Internal Links per Article, Natural Anchor Text Variety

---

## 1. Internal Linking Governance & Best Practices

Internal links are the foundation of topical authority. They establish semantic relationships between core pillar guides and supporting cluster articles, distribute crawl equity, and keep readers engaged across learning journeys.

### Core Rules:
1. **Target Density:** Every published article must incorporate **3 to 6 genuinely context-relevant internal links**.
2. **Anchor Text Diversity:** Never use the exact same phrase across multiple internal links. Alternate between descriptive noun phrases, situational prompts, and topical questions.
   * ✅ *Good:* "before sending your application, review our [comprehensive Nigerian CV formatting guide](URL)"
   * ✅ *Good:* "graduates with a second-class lower degree should review these [proven 2:2 career transition strategies](URL)"
   * ❌ *Bad:* "click here", "read more", "this post", or repeating "Nigerian jobs" 10 times.
3. **No Forced Links:** If an article has no natural connection to a topic, do not force an unnatural link. Links must add immediate, logical value to the reader.

## 6. Inter-Cluster Bridge Architecture

To prevent isolated content silos, dedicated bridges connect adjacent clusters:

* **Bridge 1 (Cluster 1 ↔ Cluster 3):** Job Scam Verification links to Graduate Salary Guide ("Unrealistic salaries exceeding ₦600k/month for entry-level data entry are primary red flags for recruitment fraud").
* **Bridge 2 (Cluster 2 ↔ Cluster 4):** NYSC Career Guide links to Digital Skills Guide ("Corps members deployed to rural PPAs can utilize their downtime to master high-value remote technical skills").
* **Bridge 3 (Cluster 3 ↔ Cluster 2):** Salary Negotiation Guide links to Professional Certifications ("Candidates possessing recognized credentials like CIPM or ICAN negotiate from a position of verified competency").
